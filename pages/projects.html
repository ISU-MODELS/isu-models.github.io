NASA Earth Science

XWEPPCloud

ROSES 2025 A.7 WATERQ · 25-WATERQ25-0061 · three-year Type 1 award

University of Idaho leads this NASA application; Iowa State is the GPU modeling subcontract. The work builds a web decision-support layer on GPU-accelerated XWEPP, WEPP-WQ, and WEPPCloud, using NASA Earth observations to simulate hydrology, sediment, and nutrients in fire-susceptible forested lakesheds around Coeur d’Alene and Hayden, Idaho.

ISU role: GPU XWEPP / XWEPPCloud development and validation (McGehee). Lead PI: Anurag Srivastava, University of Idaho.

USDA-NRCS

Climate Projections and Forecasts

Cooperative agreement NR243A750008C001 · 2023–2026

Building Understanding and Capacity for the Use of Climate Projections in Conservation Planning and Implementation. The agreement gives NRCS a working basis for climate forecasts and projections in conservation design: cover-crop timing, ENSO-informed snow and streamflow analogs, climate-enhanced topographic wetness, and agricultural waste-management climate inputs.

ISU role: PI (McGehee), with NRCS Science & Technology.

USDA-NRCS · University of Missouri

Soil Vulnerability Index

ISU subcontract · through 31 August 2026

Iowa State is the modeling and software subrecipient on Missouri’s NRCS project to improve SVI runoff classification. The lab runs WEPP sediment-loss simulations across CEAP and additional watersheds, refines rainfall-aware SVI rules, and folds those rules into the Agricultural Conservation Planning Framework.

ISU role: WEPP modeling and ACPF software (McGehee). Prime: University of Missouri.

USDA-NRCS · Ducks Unlimited

Working Lands for Resilient Communities

Regional Conservation Partnership Program · Scott County, Iowa · 2024–2029

Ducks Unlimited leads this RCPP Alternative Funding Arrangement for targeted conservation in Scott County watersheds (Upper Duck Creek, Lost Creek, and Hickory Creek). Iowa State assesses environmental outcomes of stacked in-field, edge-of-field, and wetland practices: monitoring, nested process models, and practice-to-watershed scaling.

ISU role: McMillan leads the ISU team; McGehee leads model development and implementation.
