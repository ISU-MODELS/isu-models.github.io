NASA-ROSES-A7

Project

Leveraging NASA Earth Observations for High-Resolution, GPU-Accelerated Modeling of Water Quality Dynamics in Fire-Susceptible Forested Watersheds

Summary

University of Idaho leads this NASA application; Iowa State is the GPU modeling subcontract. The work builds a web decision-support layer on GPU-accelerated XWEPP, WEPP-WQ, and WEPPCloud, using NASA Earth observations to simulate hydrology, sediment, and nutrients in fire-susceptible forested lakesheds around Coeur d’Alene and Hayden, Idaho. ISU role: GPU XWEPP / XWEPPCloud development and validation (McGehee). Lead PI: Anurag Srivastava, University of Idaho. Proposal 25-WATERQ25-0061, 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2029.

USDA-NRCS

Project

Building Understanding and Capacity for the Use of Climate Projections in Conservation Planning and Implementation

Summary

The agreement gives NRCS a working basis for climate forecasts and projections in conservation design: cover-crop timing, ENSO-informed snow and streamflow analogs, climate-enhanced topographic wetness, and agricultural waste-management climate inputs. ISU role: PI (McGehee), with NRCS Science & Technology. Cooperative agreement NR243A750008C001, 1 October 2023 – 30 September 2025 in the vita, carried on the site through 2026.

University of Missouri

Project

CEAP Watershed Assessment Study: Relating Rainfall and Erosion Index to Modern Rainfall Characteristics for Improved Classification of the Soil Vulnerability Index

Summary

Iowa State is the modeling and software subrecipient on Missouri’s NRCS project to improve SVI runoff classification. The lab runs WEPP sediment-loss simulations across CEAP and additional watersheds, refines rainfall-aware SVI rules, and folds those rules into the Agricultural Conservation Planning Framework. ISU role: PI (McGehee). Sponsor path in the vita: University of Missouri via USDA-NRCS. 1 January 2026 – 31 August 2026.

Sponsor

Ducks Unlimited

Project

Working Lands for Resilient Communities

Summary

Ducks Unlimited leads this RCPP Alternative Funding Arrangement for targeted conservation in Scott County watersheds (Upper Duck Creek, Lost Creek, and Hickory Creek). Iowa State assesses environmental outcomes of stacked in-field, edge-of-field, and wetland practices: monitoring, nested process models, and practice-to-watershed scaling. ISU role: McMillan leads the ISU team; McGehee leads model development and implementation. Sponsor path in the vita: Ducks Unlimited via USDA-NRCS. 1 May 2024 – 30 April 2027.
